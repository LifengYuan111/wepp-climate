# GPCC — SI

Projected GPCC climate forcing with storm intensification represented during the
GPCC downscaling process.

The historical method selected 25 GCMs from 52 CMIP5 downscaled datasets based
on agreement with observed changes in extreme precipitation. GPCC represented
changes in extreme storms by adjusting precipitation variance and skewness; the
skewness coefficient was related linearly to the ratio of the 99.9th-percentile
precipitation to mean daily precipitation.

The WEPP runner consumes the resulting climate files and does not calculate SI
internally.
